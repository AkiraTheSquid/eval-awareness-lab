# ARENA · Evaluation Awareness

This private Study module covers Goodfire’s paper and the supplied research guide. Its sections cover what the monitor measures, how to read the results, and how to test the interpretation. Worked cases and practice build the reasoning needed to interpret the evidence. Advanced replication and experiment execution are outside the module’s scope.

## Practice

The module has 14 lessons, 37 free-response questions, 12 math multiple-choice questions, and two staged problems that pair calculations with interpretation. Study records math and explanation as distinct facets. Strong prerequisites require direct practice evidence before later concepts unlock.

- Calculate conditional phrase probabilities and the peak monitor score across inspected boundaries.
- Calculate ranking AUROC with wins and half-credit ties.
- Calculate the chance of seeing a flag across repeated independent rollouts.

## Files

- `course.yaml` defines order and direct-evidence prerequisite settings.
- Sections contain concept Markdown and native Study question files.
- `_sources/` archives references, with citations resolved through `sources.json` and `passages.json`.
- `authoring/` stores scope, graph checks, terminology declarations, and the Jev receipt.
- The original PNG figures are bundled in `Local_Deployed_Shared/conceptual/tutor/figures/arena-eval-awareness/`.

The ARENA dashboard shows a separate module card for signed-in accounts with access. The local reading copy is `~/Downloads/arena-evaluation-awareness.html`.
